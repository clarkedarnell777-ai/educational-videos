# Social publishing package — Selling a Rental in November? Your 1031 Clock Shrinks

Figures: IRS 2026 Instructions for Form 8824 (published Nov 9, 2026): a 1031 exchange applies only to real property held for business or investment; the replacement must be identified within 45 days of the sale and received within 180 days or by the due date (including extensions) of the tax return for the year of the sale, whichever is earlier. 2026 individual returns are due April 15, 2027 for most people (IRS draft 2026 Form 4868); an extension runs to October 15, 2027. Illustrative example (dates computed): a sale on Nov 17, 2026 → day 45 = Jan 1, 2027; day 180 = May 16, 2027; April 15, 2027 is day 149, so 31 days are lost without an extension. Any calendar-year individual's sale after Oct 17, 2026 is cut short this way.

**Description:**
A 1031 exchange defers tax on a rental's gain when you reinvest in real estate. You have 45 days to identify the replacement and 180 days to close, or until your tax return due date if that comes first. Illustrative: sell Nov 17, 2026 and day 180 is May 16, 2027, but returns are due April 15, 2027. That's 31 days gone unless you extend. Talk to a tax professional before you sell.
