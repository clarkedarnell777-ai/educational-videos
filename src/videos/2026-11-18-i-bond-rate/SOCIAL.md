# Social publishing package — Savings Bonds Pay 5.51%. Here's the Part You Keep.

Figures: Series I savings bonds issued Nov 1, 2026 – Apr 30, 2027 earn a 5.51% composite rate = 1.40% fixed rate (set for the life of the bond) + a semiannual inflation rate of 2.04% (CPI-U, Mar→Sep 2026). Composite formula per 31 CFR 359.14; the inflation portion is about 4.11%. Rates announced Oct 30, 2026 (reported by Tipswatch, Doctor of Credit and Wolf Street; TreasuryDirect is the official source). I bonds can't be redeemed for 12 months; cashing out before 5 years forfeits the last 3 months of interest. Illustrative: $10,000 × 5.51% ÷ 2 ≈ $275.50 over six months; the rate resets in May 2027.

**Title:** I Bonds Pay 5.51% — But Only One Part Is Yours for Life

**Description:**
I bonds issued Nov 2026–Apr 2027 pay 5.51%: a 1.40% fixed rate locked for the life of the bond, plus about 4.1% from inflation that resets every six months. Illustrative: $10,000 earns about $275 in six months. You can't cash out for 12 months, and before 5 years you give up 3 months of interest. Before buying, check the fixed rate — that's the part you keep.

Sources: TreasuryDirect I bond rates (Nov 2026–Apr 2027, announced Oct 30, 2026); BLS CPI-U via FRED; 31 CFR 359.
