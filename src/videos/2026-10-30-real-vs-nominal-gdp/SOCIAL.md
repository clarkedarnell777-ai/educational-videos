Figures: BEA, GDP (Advance Estimate) 3rd Quarter 2026, Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep), published Oct 29, 2026. Advance estimate; BEA's second estimate is due Nov 25, 2026. Paycheck example is illustrative.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** GDP Grew 5.6%… or 3.4%? Real vs. Nominal Explained

**Description:**
The U.S. economy grew 5.6% in Q3 2026, or 3.4%, depending on which number you read. Both are official. Nominal GDP counts spending in today's dollars; real GDP removes price increases. In the BEA's advance estimate (Oct 29, 2026), nominal GDP grew 5.6% at an annual rate and real GDP 3.4%; the gap was mostly higher prices. Same idea for your paycheck: a 5% raise with 3% inflation buys about 2% more (illustrative).

Source: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, GDP Advance Estimate Q3 2026 (bea.gov). Figures will be revised Nov 25, 2026.
