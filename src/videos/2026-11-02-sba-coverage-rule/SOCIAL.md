## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Buying a Business With an SBA Loan? Your Forecast Won't Save the Deal

**Description:**
Since Oct 1, 2026, most buyers using an SBA 7(a) loan to acquire a business need 1.25x debt service coverage, up from 1.15x, and it's tested on past earnings, not projections. Illustrative math: every $100 of loan payments needs $125 of earnings. A $500,000 deal needs $50,000 down (10% of project costs), and seller financing can cover half of that at most.

Sources: SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 (effective Oct 1, 2026); Starfield & Smith, Sep 9, 2026; PilieroMazza, Aug 24, 2026; Matthews, Sep 25, 2026.
