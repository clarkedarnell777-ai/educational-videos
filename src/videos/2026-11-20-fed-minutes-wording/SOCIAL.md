# Social publishing package — The Fed Didn't Hike. Why Are Borrowers Bracing?

Figures: The Fed held its target range at 3.75%–4.00% on Oct 28, 2026 (vote 10–2). Minutes of the Oct 27–28 meeting, released Nov 18, 2026, say "most participants" supported holding, "some participants" favored a 25-basis-point increase, and "almost all participants" judged that, under their baseline outlooks, further increases would likely be appropriate — expectations, not decisions. Next meeting: Dec 8–9, 2026. Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year fixed: 7.11% (Nov 13) → 7.28% (Nov 19) → 7.21% (Nov 25, 2026); 6.23% a year ago. Illustrative: $300,000, 30-yr P&I, $2,018 at 7.11% vs $2,053 at 7.28% (+$35/mo). Mortgage rates are shown alongside the minutes, not as caused by them.
