# Social publishing package — Your Card Debt Doubles in 3 Years. Your Savings? 180.

Figures: Federal Reserve G.19 (released Nov 6, 2026): commercial-bank credit card rate on accounts assessed interest was 22.36% in Q3 2026 (preliminary). FDIC national average savings rate: 0.38% (October 2026). NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Report (Nov 10, 2026): US credit card balances $1.29 trillion in Q3 2026. Doubling times (~3.1 years vs ~182 years) and the $5,000 example (≈$1,240 vs ≈$19 of interest in a year) are illustrative: monthly compounding, no payments, no new charges or fees, rates held constant.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Card Debt Can Double in ~3 Years. Savings Take 180+.

**Description:**
At 22.36%, the average rate banks charged on card accounts that paid interest in Q3 2026 (Fed G.19, preliminary), an unpaid balance would double in about 3.1 years with monthly compounding and no payments. At the 0.38% national average savings rate (FDIC, Oct 2026), money takes about 182 years to double. That's compound interest working for or against you. Illustrative: $5,000 left on a card for a year ≈ $1,240 in interest; in savings ≈ $19.

Sources: Federal Reserve G.19, Nov 6, 2026; FDIC national rate (Oct 2026, via FRED); NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Report, Q3 2026.
