# Social publishing package — 534,947 New Business Applications. When Does One Break Even?

Figures: U.S. Census Bureau, Business Formation Statistics, September 2026, seasonally adjusted, released Oct 14, 2026. The 29,022 projected employer businesses is Census's forward projection for the September application cohort, not a count, and many applications are never meant to hire, so it is not a failure rate. The coffee cart is illustrative.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** 534,947 New Business Applications. When Does One Break Even?

**Description:**
Americans filed 534,947 business applications in September 2026, per the Census Bureau. Before you become one of them, know your break-even: monthly fixed costs divided by what each sale leaves after its own cost. Illustrative coffee cart: $3,000 a month in fixed costs, $5 cups that cost $2 to make, so $3 left per cup and 1,000 cups a month to break even (about 34 a day). Census projects about 1 in 18 of September's applications will have payroll within a year.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Business Formation Statistics, Sep 2026, released Oct 14, 2026. Coffee cart example is illustrative.
Educational only, not financial advice.
