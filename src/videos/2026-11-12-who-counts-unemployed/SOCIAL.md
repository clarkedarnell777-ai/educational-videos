# Social publishing package — 4.1% Unemployed? Who the Number Leaves Out

Figures: BLS Employment Situation for October 2026 (released Nov 6, 2026), household survey, seasonally adjusted. Official unemployment rate (U-3) 4.1% (September: 4.2%): 7.068 million unemployed out of a 170.683 million civilian labor force. Broader U-6 rate 7.9% (September: 7.6%), which adds all marginally attached workers (including discouraged workers) and 4.84 million people working part time for economic reasons. Definition: BLS counts someone as unemployed if they had no job in the survey week, were available for work, and actively looked in the prior 4 weeks (or were on temporary layoff). Figures are subject to annual revision.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** 4.1% Unemployed? Here's Who That Number Leaves Out

**Description:**
The official unemployment rate was 4.1% in October 2026: 7.1 million unemployed out of a 170.7 million labor force (BLS, seasonally adjusted). To count, you need no job, availability, and a job search in the past 4 weeks. People who want work but stopped looking, and part-timers who want full-time hours, show up in the broader U-6 rate instead: 7.9% the same month. Check both on jobs day.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Situation, October 2026 (released Nov 6, 2026), Tables A-1 and A-15.
