# Social publishing package — Two Fed Officials Wanted a Hike. Does Your Loan Float?

Figures: On Oct 28, 2026 the Fed held its federal funds target range at 3.75%–4.00% by a 10–2 vote; Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack preferred a quarter-point increase (Federal Reserve statement, Oct 28, 2026). The bank prime loan rate is 7.00% (Fed H.15 via FRED DPRIME, Nov 23, 2026) — 3.00 points above the top of the Fed range, an observed gap rather than a rule. HELOC variable rates are an index such as prime plus a lender margin (CFPB HELOC booklet). Illustrative: interest on a constant $50,000 balance rises about $125 a year (~$10.42/month) for each 0.25-point increase. Next FOMC meeting: Dec 8–9, 2026.
