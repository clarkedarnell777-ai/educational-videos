# Social publishing package — The Nasdaq Just Hit a Correction. What Does That Mean?

Figures: NASDAQ Composite daily closes via FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), Oct 6 to Nov 5, 2026. "Correction" (10%+ below a high) and "bear market" (20%+) are common conventions, not official definitions. The $10,000 example is illustrative and uses the price index only (no fees, dividends or taxes).

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** The Nasdaq Just Hit a Correction. What Does That Mean?

**Description:**
The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.52% on Thursday, Nov 5, 2026, closing at 24,315.00. That's 11.90% below its record close of 27,599.89 on Oct 6, past the 10% line most people use to call a correction. A bear market usually means 20% or more. Illustrative: $10,000 tracking the index at the peak would be about $8,810 at Thursday's close. Before reacting, ask when you'll actually need the money.
