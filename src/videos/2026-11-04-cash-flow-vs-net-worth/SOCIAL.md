# Social publishing package — Worth $200K, Still Short on Bills? Cash Flow vs Net Worth

Figures: Federal Reserve, 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances, published Oct 9, 2026, in 2025 dollars. The figures are separate survey measures, not one family. The median balance is among families holding transaction accounts. The overspending example is illustrative.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Worth $200K, Still Short on Bills? Cash Flow vs Net Worth

**Description:**
Net worth is a snapshot: what you own minus what you owe. Cash flow is the movie: money in, money out. The Fed's 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances shows a median family net worth of $215,900, a median transaction account balance of $8,200, and 19.6% of families reporting late loan payments. This month, subtract spending from income and track that number.

Source: Federal Reserve, 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances, published Oct 9, 2026. Separate measures, not one family. Overspending example is illustrative.
Educational only, not financial advice.
