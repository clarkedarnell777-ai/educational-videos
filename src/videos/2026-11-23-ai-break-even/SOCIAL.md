# Social publishing package — 1 in 5 Businesses Use AI. When Does It Pay Off?

Figures: 19.8% of U.S. businesses reported using AI (Census Bureau Business Trends and Outlook Survey, national rate, collection period ending May 3, 2026). 37% of firms with 250+ employees vs. under 20% of firms with 1–4 employees (Census BTOS, Dec 2025–May 2026). About 96% of AI-using firms reported no change in total employment over the prior six months (BTOS AI supplement, April 2026, as reported by the Minneapolis Fed on May 29, 2026). Illustrative example, not data: a $30/month tool with time valued at $25/hour breaks even at 1.2 hours saved per month; 8 hours saved = $200 of time, $170/month net.

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** 1 in 5 Businesses Use AI. When Does It Pay Off?

**Description:**
About 1 in 5 U.S. businesses (19.8%) reported using AI in spring 2026, per the Census Bureau. For a small business, an AI tool is an operating cost that has to pay for itself. Illustrative math: a $30/month tool, your time worth $25/hour → break even at 1.2 hours saved a month. And it's rarely about cutting staff: about 96% of AI-using firms reported no headcount change. Try it: pick one repetitive task, test a tool for 30 days, track the hours.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau BTOS (period ending May 3, 2026; story revised May 22, 2026); Minneapolis Fed (May 29, 2026). Break-even example is illustrative.
Educational only, not financial advice.
