## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** How Banks Really Make Money: The Interest Rate Spread

**Description:**
The average U.S. savings account pays 0.38% (FDIC, Oct 2026). Bank credit cards average about 21% APR (Federal Reserve G.19, Q1 2026). That gap is the spread — and across all their loans, U.S. banks earned a 3.32% net interest margin in Q2 2026 (FDIC). Two ways to get on the right side: compare your savings APY and pay card balances in full.

Sources: FDIC national rates (fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SNDR) · Federal Reserve G.19 · FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile Q2 2026.
