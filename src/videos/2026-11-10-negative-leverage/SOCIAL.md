# Social publishing package — Can a Mortgage Make a Rental Lose Money?

Figures: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, 30-year fixed average 7.18% for the week of Nov 5, 2026 (published Nov 5, 2026). PMMS is an owner-occupied rate; investment-property loans usually cost more. The $300,000 rental is illustrative; all math is computed (pre-tax, year 1, ignoring closing costs, principal paydown and appreciation).

## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Can a Mortgage Make a Rental Lose Money?

**Description:**
A loan is supposed to boost a rental's return, but not when the loan costs more than the property earns. Cap rate = yearly income after expenses ÷ price. Your loan has its own rate: yearly payments ÷ the loan. At Freddie Mac's 7.18% average 30-year rate (Nov 5, 2026), that's about 8.1%. Illustrative: a $300,000 rental with $18,000 of yearly income (a 6% cap rate), bought with 25% down, pays $18,291 a year on the loan, about $291 more than it brings in. Before you borrow, make sure the cap rate beats your loan's cost.

Source: Freddie Mac PMMS, week of Nov 5, 2026. Example is illustrative; pre-tax, year 1.
Educational only, not financial advice.
